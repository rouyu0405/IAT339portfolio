UX Design Case Study

Interactive
Zone Map

Redesigning the TransLink SkyTrain fare map to simplify the zone-based system for new and infrequent riders.

Year: Spring 2025
Built for: IAT 333 - Interaction Design Methods
In collaboration with: Irina N. / Max N. / Mikael G.
Role: Lead UX Researcher & Designer
Skills & Tools: Research / Interview / Figma / Film Editing / Adobe After Effects

Objective

The existing TransLink SkyTrain fare system presents a significant cognitive load for infrequent riders. The "Fare Zone" logic, which fluctuates based on time-of-day and day-of-week, creates high friction at the point of purchase.

→

Outcome

An interactive zone map that can create a smoother ticket purchasing experience for low-frequency transit riders through research from interviews, workshops, and critiques with various stakeholders.
