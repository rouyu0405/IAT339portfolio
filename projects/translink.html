01

UX Design Case Study

Interactive
Zone Map

Redesigning the TransLink SkyTrain fare map to simplify the zone-based system for new and infrequent riders.

Year: Spring 2025

Built for: IAT 333 - Interaction Design Methods

In collaboration with: Irina N. / Max N. / Mikael G.

Role: Lead UX Researcher & Designer

Skills&Tools: Research / Interview / Figma / Film Editing / Adobe After Effects

objective

The existing TransLink SkyTrain fare system presents a significant cognitive load for infrequent riders. The "Fare Zone" logic, which fluctuates based on time-of-day and day-of-week, creates high friction at the point of purchase.

→

outcome

An interactive zone map that can create a smoother ticket purchasing experience for low-frequency transit riders through research from interviews, workshops, and critques with various stakeholders.

my roles

01.

UX Research
Led short surveys with passengers at Metrotown Station.

02.

Video Lead
Directed the user journey video to bridge wireframes and physical reality.

03.

Collaboration
Facilitated co-creation workshops to align design decisions with stakeholder goals.

the process

Survey Sampling

Conducted field research at Metrotown Station, surveying 10 users interacting with CVMs. We identified that users frequently looked back and forth between the screen and static map, causing "navigation loops."
